Selected work · Case study

Eden Mill: rebuilding a brand from the inside out.

How Liquid developed a complete commercial intervention and execution blueprint for one of Scotland's most ambitious distilleries.

Eden Mill came to us at a pivotal moment. A distillery with genuine provenance, a passionate team, and a product range that had outgrown its brand. The challenge was not awareness — it was coherence. The brand needed a strategic overhaul that connected its heritage to a modern, premium positioning without losing what made it distinctive.

Commercial Intervention

We audited Eden Mill's entire brand ecosystem — packaging, pricing architecture, channel strategy, competitive positioning, and digital presence. The result identified exactly where the brand was leaking value and where the growth opportunities sat. A forensic examination of a drinks brand's commercial health, informed by 17 years of category experience and real market data.

Execution Blueprints

Strategy without execution is a PowerPoint deck. We delivered detailed blueprints covering visual identity refinement, packaging redesign direction, content strategy, trade marketing activation, and a phased rollout plan. Each blueprint included timelines, budget frameworks and measurable KPIs — not vague creative ambitions, but a roadmap the team could act on immediately.

Market Intelligence Integration

Every recommendation was backed by data from Liquid Economy. We showed Eden Mill exactly where Scottish whisky and gin were growing, which price segments were gaining share, and how their competitors were positioning. The strategy was not built on instinct — it was built on evidence.

The Outcome

A distillery with a clear strategic direction, a differentiated brand position, and a detailed plan to execute against it. Eden Mill is the proof of what happens when deep category knowledge meets rigorous creative thinking.

"Strategy without execution is a PowerPoint deck."
About

Seventeen years in the glass.

Liquid is built by someone who has been inside the drinks industry longer than most brands have existed.

Founded by James Huertas — a creative director, photographer, and drinks industry strategist with 17 years of experience working with some of the most respected names in the business. The journey started behind the camera, shooting for premium drinks brands and building a visual language for the category. It evolved into consulting for LVMH, working inside the brand architecture of the world's largest luxury group.

Liquid was born — not as another generalist agency chasing any client with a budget, but as a specialist partner built exclusively for the drinks industry. Every tool, every process, every piece of intelligence is designed for one sector and one sector only.
